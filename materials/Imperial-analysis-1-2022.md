<div class="content-card">
  <h1>Syllabus:</h1>
  <ul>
    <li>Real Numbers: The archimedean property, density of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\mathbb{R}$, completeness axiom, Sup and Inf, decimal expansions, countability and uncountability.</li>
    <li>Real and complex sequences: Convergence and divergence, sandwich test, subsequences, monotonic sequences, $\limsup$ and $\liminf$, Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem, Cauchy sequences, general principle of convergence.</li>
    <li>Real and complex series: Convergent and absolutely convergent series, comparison test for non-negative series and absolutely convergent series, alternating series test, rearranging absolutely convergent series, radius of convergence of power series, exponential series.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
