  <h1>First Year Project:</h1>
  <p class="intro-text">
    <span class="drop-cap">T</span>his is a poster project completed during my first year of undergraduate studies at <strong>Imperial College London</strong>. 
  </p>

  <p>
    The project explores the mathematical modeling of solitons in shallow water via the <strong>Korteweg-de Vries (KdV) equation</strong>. This specific model accounts for the unique phenomenon where waves maintain their shape while propagating at constant speeds. 
  </p>
  
  <p>
    Throughout this study, I examined both the analytical properties—such as the balance between dispersion and non-linearity—and the numerical behaviors of solitary wave solutions.
  </p>
