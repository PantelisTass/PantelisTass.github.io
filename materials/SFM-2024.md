<div class="content-card">
  <h1>Syllabus:</h1>
  <ul>
    <li>Standing Assumptions and Notation: Assumptions about financial markets and asset prices; definitions of market setup.</li>
    <li>One-Period Model: Investor wealth and random asset prices; mean-variance analysis and portfolio optimization.</li>
    <li>Market Portfolio and Mutual Fund Theorem: Market portfolio definition; efficient frontier formulation.</li>
    <li>Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM): Assumptions and derivation; risk-return relationship.</li>
    <li>Expected Utility Hypothesis: Utility functions, preferences, and risk aversion measures.</li>
    <li>State Price Densities: Definition, derivation, and relationship to utility maximization.</li>
    <li>Risk Neutral Measures: Concept, applications in pricing, and equivalent measures.</li>
    <li>Arbitrage and Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing: Arbitrage definition and implications; fundamental theorem proof.</li>
    <li>Utility Maximization in Binomial Models: Optimal portfolios and contingent claim pricing.</li>
    <li>Multi-Period Models: Filtrations, adapted processes, and conditional expectations.</li>
    <li>Stopping Times and Optional Stopping Theorem: Definitions, applications, and proofs.</li>
    <li>Martingale Theory: Martingale transforms, examples, and properties.</li>
    <li>Pricing and Hedging of Derivatives: Attainable claims, no-arbitrage prices, and examples.</li>
    <li>Advanced Topics: Filtrations, sigma-algebras, and conditional probability.</li>
  </ul>
