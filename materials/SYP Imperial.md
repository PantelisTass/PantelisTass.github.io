  <h1>Project Overview:</h1>
  <p>
    <span class="drop-cap">T</span>his is a report of our group’s study of <strong>Darboux Transformations</strong> and its role in the spectral analysis of one-dimensional Schrödinger operators. 
  </p>
  
  <ul>
    <li>Developed the Hilbert space formalism for spectral analysis.</li>
    <li>Applied the method to three Schrödinger operator eigenvalue problems:
      <ul>
        <li>The simple harmonic oscillator.</li>
        <li>The reflectionless potential.</li>
        <li>The Coulomb potential.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Explored broad implications in quantum mechanics and mathematical physics.</li>
  </ul>
