  <h1>Research Project Overview:</h1>
  <p class="intro-text">
    <span class="drop-cap">T</span>hese are notes from my summer research project conducted during the third year of my undergraduate studies at <strong>Imperial College London</strong> (Summer 2023), under the supervision of <strong>Professor Greg Pavliotis</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    The project focused on the <strong>Mathematics of Machine Learning</strong>, specifically investigating the mean-field limits of neural networks. A key observation was the convergence of hidden layer parameter distributions during stochastic gradient descent to a bimodal distribution as the number $N$ of hidden units increases with the number of epochs.
  </p>
