  <p>The 49th Research Students' Conference in Probability and Statistics was held in Cambridge (UK) from 31 August to 4 September 2026. The conference brings together PhD and predoctoral researchers in probability, statistics and related fields from across the UK and Europe. </p>

<p>I was part of the organising committee and was responsible for the Probability section of the conference.</p>

<p>I gave a talk on recent work with my advisor, Sourav Sarkar on the Brownian regularity of the KPZ fixed point.</p>
